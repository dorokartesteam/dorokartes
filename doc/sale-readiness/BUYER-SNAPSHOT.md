# Dorokartes.gr — Buyer Investment Snapshot

## Executive summary

Dorokartes.gr is a Greek gift-card discovery platform that helps users find gift cards by brand, category and occasion, then continue to the merchant's official purchase page.

> Όλες οι δωροκάρτες της Ελλάδας. Σε ένα μέρος.

Dorokartes.gr is not the seller of the listed gift cards. Its value lies in catalog coverage, structured merchant data, SEO landing architecture, verification/provenance tooling, discoverability, and transferability.
